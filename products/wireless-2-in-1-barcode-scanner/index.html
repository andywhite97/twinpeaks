Wireless 2-in-1 Barcode Scanner

Wireless 2-in-1 Barcode Scanner

A versatile 1D barcode scanner that can be used wirelessly or connected directly via USB. Its rechargeable battery and included USB cable ensure reliable scanning even when the battery is low.

Key Features

  • 📡 Wireless & USB connectivity
  • 🔍 Supports standard 1D linear barcodes
  • ⚡ Up to 100 scans/second
  • 🔦 650nm laser for fast and accurate scanning
  • 🔋 Built-in rechargeable battery
  • 🔌 USB cable included for charging and wired use

📦 Ideal for POS, stock control, retail, and inventory management

🖥️ Cannot scan QR/2D barcodes or barcodes displayed on screens

What's Included

  • 📦 1 × Wireless Barcode Scanner
  • 🔌 1 × USB charging/data cable
  • 📡 1 × Wireless USB/Bluetooth adapter
