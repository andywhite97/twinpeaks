Our story

Connecting people, technology and progress.

TwinPeaks Investment is an Eswatini-based technology and security solutions company, providing IT solutions, security systems, POS solutions and digital services.

TwinPeaks Investment is an Eswatini-based technology and security solutions company serving homes, businesses and institutions. We design, install, configure and support practical systems that help clients protect their spaces, strengthen connectivity and operate more effectively. Our approach is solutions-led: we begin with the challenge, recommend the right combination of technology and services, and provide implementation with an emphasis on usability, reliability and ongoing support.
